VIX futures curve: trading the basis with an ES hedge

Hypothesis

VIX futures trade at a premium to spot VIX when the curve is in contango and at a discount in backwardation, and that gap tends to close as contracts approach settlement. A short VX position in steep contango (or long in backwardation) collects that roll, but carries heavy equity-market exposure.

What I built

  • Implemented the method from a published research paper that uses the shape of the VIX futures curve to size VX trades and the offsetting ES position.
  • Wrote a standalone Python backtest first to verify the signal and hedge math before committing to a framework.
  • Built a Python dashboard to inspect the term structure, daily roll and positions over time.
  • Ported the strategy to QuantConnect with continuous-contract handling and rolls.

Risk I watch

Short-volatility carry looks smooth until it doesn't. The backtest is examined through February 2018 and March 2020, when the curve flipped into backwardation in days.

International momentum: rotating across country ETFs

Hypothesis

Country equity markets that have outperformed over recent months tend to keep outperforming over the next few, because information and capital flows move across borders slowly. Ranking single-country ETFs on trailing return and holding the leaders should beat an equal-weight basket.

What I built

  • A universe of single-country ETFs, ranked on trailing return at each rebalance.
  • Holds the top-ranked markets and rotates on a fixed schedule, with trading costs modeled in LEAN.
  • Compared against an equal-weight basket of the same ETFs so the result measures selection, not just international beta.

Risk I watch

Momentum crashes in sharp reversals, and country ETFs carry currency exposure. Turnover and the benchmark comparison matter more here than headline return.
